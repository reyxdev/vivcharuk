# Client decisions — round 22: categories in the panel, the product card (2026-10-02)

Answers given in the session on 2026-10-02 to 50 questions after the remark «категорії виглядають дуже
хаотично» (the category chips in the product card). They override earlier rounds where they differ.
Codes K01–K48 follow the question numbers.

## Choosing categories in the product card

- **K01** A tree: each top category is a card that folds open; inside it, a list of its subcategories.
- **K02** One main category plus at most two additional ones (three in all).
- **K03** Only a subcategory can be chosen. A top category is choosable only when it has no subcategories.
- **K04** «Основна категорія» is its own field at the top of the picker.
- **K05** On opening, only the groups that hold a chosen category are unfolded.
- **K06** No search field. **K07** No product counts next to categories.
- **K08** The chosen categories are repeated in a line «Обрано: …», each with a remove (×) button.
- **K09** A round radio marks the main category, square checkboxes the additional ones.
- **K10** Computer: the group cards in three columns. **K11** Phone: the picker opens as a full-screen sheet.
- **K12** The hint stays an (i) icon.
- **K13** «Від партнерів» is only the checkbox on the product. It is not a category one can pick; the
  site gathers the partner group by itself from products with the checkbox.
- **K14** A product without a category cannot be published (already blocking; kept).
- **K15** The new-product steps start with the category (already so, round 20 #154; kept).
- **K16** The previous product's category is not pre-filled for the next one.
- **K17** Subcategories in the same order as in the site menu.
- **K18** Long names are shown in full and wrap.

## The categories themselves

- **K19** Make the subcategory names consistent: the developer proposes a list, the client approves it
  before anything is renamed.
- **K20** «Подушки та постіль» is split into two groups: «Подушки» and «Постіль».
- **K21** Groups without subcategories («Шкіряні сумки», «Еко-чаї») stay as they are.
- **K22** The category list is changed by the Owner and the Administrator only.
- **K23** No «+ нова категорія» in the product card; new categories only in «Категорії».
- **K24** Reordering by dragging and also by up/down arrows.
- **K25** Deleting a category that has products asks where to move them.
- **K26** No separate «seasonal» switch. (A new category still starts hidden, as before.)
- **K27** Two levels only: group → subcategory.
- **K45** The site menu uses the same tree and names as the panel.
- **K46** A picture only for the top groups.

## The product card

- **K28** «Колекції» stay, folded under «Додатково».
- **K29** «Від партнерів», «Ручна робота», «Єдиний екземпляр» become switches with icons, in their own block.
- **K30** Chosen chips in the dark theme are filled with the accent colour, not only outlined.
- **K31** One page with the section menu on the left (as now). **K32** Section order as now.
- **K33** Changes are saved to the draft automatically.
- **K34** The readiness line shows only while something is missing.
- **K35** «Опублікувати» in a sticky bar at the bottom (as now).
- **K36** Field density as now.
- **K37** «Історія змін» moves into the ⋯ menu.
- **K38** On a computer the «як на сайті» preview sits beside the editor, live.
- **K39** «Склад і характеристики» are ready fields that depend on the product type (already so through
  the templates; kept).
- **K40** A description template per product type is filled into a new product (already so, round 20
  #127; kept).
- **K41** Sizes and colours stay a table.

## The product list

- **K42** Filter by category as a tree in the «Фільтри» panel.
- **K43** The list shows the main category only.
- **K44** Bulk change of category for the selected products.
- **K48** Changes go straight into the panel (no mock-ups first).

K47, K49, K50 were left unanswered.
