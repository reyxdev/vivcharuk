# Client decisions — round 24: SEO / GEO after the audit (2026-10-03)

Answers given in the session on 2026-10-03 to 200 questions built from the audit in
`vivcharuk.com-audit/` (FULL-AUDIT-REPORT.md, score 62/100; ACTION-PLAN.md). Codes G001–G200 follow
the question numbers. Goal: the highest possible audit score **without** loading ordinary visitors
with text: the first screen stays photo, name, price, button; long text is folded under
«Детальніше»; no hidden text for search engines, ever (G098, G172, G173). Nothing invented: where the
owner has not given a fact, nothing is shown in its place (G186).

The round overrides earlier rounds where they differ. Notably **G093 overrides round 18 / D-launch
«uk only»: English is enabled at launch next to Ukrainian.**

## Technical SEO and indexing

- **G001–G003** Category address is nested `/uk/<group>/<sub>`; every other form 301s to it; product
  breadcrumbs are Головна › Група › Підкатегорія › Товар.
- **G004–G005** Empty categories: `noindex`, out of the sitemap and hidden from the site menu until
  they have a product.
- **G006** The journal is `noindex` and out of the sitemap until its first article; it stays in the menu.
- **G007** Category `lastmod` = last change of its products. **G008** Legal pages enter the sitemap
  once their final text exists (G195: with an «Оновлено» date).
- **G009–G010** `/UK/` and any upper case → 301 to lower case; one slash form, the other 301s.
- **G011–G012** Root files: `llms.txt`, `/.well-known/security.txt` (contact: the developer's address,
  set on the server), `manifest.webmanifest`, `humans.txt`, the IndexNow key file.
- **G013–G015** Full security headers on the site; CSP one week in report-only, then enforced; HSTS
  preload one month after launch.
- **G016–G018** All AI crawlers allowed on public pages; user-triggered agents (ChatGPT-User,
  Claude-User, Perplexity-User) may go through checkout; checkout lines and payment choice render on
  the server.
- **G019–G021** Site search and filters stay `noindex`; pagination stays self-canonical.
- **G022** IndexNow on. **G023–G024** Search Console verified by a DNS record at Porkbun; Bing
  Webmaster Tools imported from GSC. **G025** GA4 only after cookie consent. **G026** Own lightweight
  real-user speed measurement (LCP, INP, CLS), no cookies.
- **G041** Short server cache (60 s) of pages for anonymous visitors. **G042** No CDN.
- **G043** A useful 404 (search + popular categories). **G044** A sold-out product keeps its page
  («Немає / Під замовлення» + similar products). **G045** A deleted product 301s to its category.
- **G046** Demo products and reviews are checked for and removed on the server before launch.
- **G047** No daily 404/500 digest. **G048** The ticker stays; its duplicated copy is hidden from
  screen readers.
- **G049, G199** The site opens to everyone right after phase 1 (duplicates, sitemap, placeholders,
  security headers). **G050** Re-audit on production after phase 1 and after phase 2.

## The owner's family history (supplied 2026-10-03)

Іван's own text «Наша історія» is published whole on «Про нас» (Ukrainian as written, English translated
by the developer), with a «Коротко в датах» list. Facts it establishes:

- Owner: **Іван Федорович Гондурак**; in the craft since **1972**, starting with ліжники (his parents wove them).
- Late 1980s: one of the first cooperatives in the district, «Прикарпаття», later «Калина» (ліжники, wool
  and leather goods, wood, metalwork and embossing).
- 1987: married; the work becomes a family business. After 1991: renamed **«Вівчарик»**; carding machines
  bought; yarn and warp for ліжники; people bring wool for carding and still buy **ровниця** to spin at home.
- Later: knitting machines (wool sweaters) and quilting machines (wool blankets, коци).
- 2003–2004: a sheepskin processing workshop (tanned skins; sheepskin rugs).
- Today: part of the lines passed to his children; Іван kept the shop and the production — **ліжники,
  коци, and bed linen sewn in the own sewing room**.
- «Понад п'ятдесят років роботи, два покоління майстрів» is the story's own wording about the craft; the
  approved tagline («Понад 30 років виробляємо…») stays unchanged and exact.
- JSON-LD Organization gets `founder` (Person). Still no `foundingDate` (G111): the story gives «після 1991»,
  not a date.
- Open question for Іван (checklist): which of the claimed in-house stages (вичинка шкур, валяння,
  в'язання) are still done by Вівчарик after some lines went to the children.
